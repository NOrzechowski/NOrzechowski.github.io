I’m a software engineer and technical lead at [Citi](https://citi.com), where I work on large-scale Java [Spark](https://spark.apache.org/) applications that process capital markets data. More recently, I’ve also been building agentic AI applications using RAG, PgVector, and MCPs.

My background spans music, science, engineering, and problem-solving. Growing up homeschooled, I devoted a lot of time to music: training classically in violin and piano, teaching private students of all ages, and serving as music teacher at a local Montessori school for grades K–8.

My interest in programming began in high school, when I took part in an Advanced Studies Program for high school students that offered a six-week intensive AI course. There, I wrote my first computer program (the Towers of Hanoi in LISP), built a robot to compete in sumo wrestling, and discovered my love for coding. Later, in a dual-degree program between Clark University and Columbia University, I earned a BA in Computer Science with a Mathematics minor and a concentration in Computational Science, along with a BS in Electrical Engineering with a specialization in Digital Signal Processing.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked across a range of industries and roles—from building SaaS solutions for shipping logistics and consulting on backend systems for vehicle rental agencies, to launching a first-to-market healthtech application in hospital systems and bringing mission-critical, data-intensive applications into production.
